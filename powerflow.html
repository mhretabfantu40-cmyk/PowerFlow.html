Fekadu BYD, run for drivers and workshops who need it right

We supply BYD-compatible spare parts and back them with the inspection, charging and diagnostic work an EV actually needs — not just the box on the shelf.

Most of our customers are drivers who bought their first electric car and workshops who are still building up EV expertise. We fill both gaps: parts when you need them, and know-how when you don't yet have it in-house.

  • Genuine, compatibility-checked components
  • Pre-purchase and resale inspections
  • Charging setup and NFC access support
  • Practical training for everyday EV drivers

What we handle

EV spare parts

Compatible components and accessories for BYD electric vehicles and the workshops that service them.

Diagnostic software
